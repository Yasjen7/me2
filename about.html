Yasmeen

Artist • Developer • Mom • Educator

Hello! I'm Yasmeen Vargas 👋

I am a 25-year-old artist, born and raised in the Bronx, and a proud mom to my amazing 3-year-old son. I am passionate about community building that encourages better ways of living.

With a deep curiosity for both art and technology, I'm driven to explore how they can coexist to uplift us and future generations. My journey into tech began with a cybersecurity bootcamp, and I've never looked back.

I'm passionate about educating others on essential tech hygiene practices to help protect their devices and data, especially when it comes to children. Watching my son navigate technology has been a powerful reminder of how important it is to instill these habits early on.

I'm always eager to learn, grow, and create spaces that inspire positive change while ensuring that the next generation has the tools and knowledge to navigate their digital future safely and creatively.

My Journey

Growing Up in NYC

Growing up in the NYC, the LES and the Bronx shaped my perspective on community, resilience, and creativity. As an artist, I've always been drawn to expressing the vibrancy and strength of my community through various mediums. The diverse culture and strong community bonds taught me the importance of giving back and lifting each other up.

Artistic Journey

Art has always been my first language. From childhood sketches to digital masterpieces, I've explored various mediums including mixed media, digital illustration, and interactive installations. My art often reflects themes of community, identity, and the intersection of tradition and technology.

Motherhood Transformation

Becoming a mother at 22 was a transformative experience that deepened my commitment to creating positive change. Watching my son grow up in a digital world made me realize how crucial it is to understand technology not just as users, but as informed advocates for safe digital practices.

Tech Education & Cybersecurity

My transition into technology wasn't planned—it was born from necessity and curiosity. The cybersecurity bootcamp opened my eyes to the intersection of art and technology, where creativity meets problem-solving. I discovered that the same attention to detail and creative thinking that drives my art could be applied to understanding and securing digital spaces.

Current Mission

Today, I'm committed to bridging the gap between artistic expression and technological empowerment, particularly in communities that have been historically underrepresented in tech. I believe that when we combine creativity with technological literacy, we create opportunities for transformative change.
